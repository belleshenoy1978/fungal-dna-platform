Automated fungal DNA analysis for Indian coastal samples

Upload ITS / 18S amplicon reads or single ITS barcode sequences. The pipeline trims primers (cutadapt), merges, quality-filters, denoises and removes chimeras (VSEARCH), assigns taxonomy against UNITE v10 with SINTAX, and publishes the results to a public Google Drive folder and a live ledger that feeds the dashboard below.{% if static_mode | default(false) %} In free mode, submissions arrive through a Google Form and are processed by a public GitHub Actions workflow; a typical job takes 5–40 minutes.{% endif %} No {% if static_mode | default(false) %}platform {% endif %}account is needed; everything submitted here is public.

Amplicon analysis

Paired or single-end Illumina FASTQ(.gz) for ITS1, ITS2, 18S-V4 or 18S-V9 (or custom primers). Output: ASV table with taxonomy, read-survival QC per sample, Shannon diversity, composition charts and downloadable TSV/FASTA files.

Submit amplicon data →

DNA barcoding

Paste or upload Sanger ITS sequences (FASTA or .ab1). Each query is searched against UNITE species hypotheses (VSEARCH global alignment) and classified with SINTAX; you get identity, coverage, SH, confidence and a printable report.
