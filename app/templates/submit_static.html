Submit data for analysis

One Google Form serves both analysis types. Choose the analysis type in the form, attach your files (or paste sequences), and submit. The job is queued automatically, processed by a public GitHub Actions workflow, and its results appear on the dashboard and on a result page (job.html?id=<job_id>) within minutes to about an hour. No platform account is needed (Google Forms itself asks for a Google sign-in for file uploads); everything submitted here is public.

Amplicon analysis (metabarcoding)

Input: demultiplexed Illumina FASTQ or FASTQ.gz, one or two files per sample (R1/R2 are paired by filename: _R1/_R2 or _1/_2). Markers: ITS1 (ITS1F/ITS2), ITS2 (ITS3/ITS4 or ITS86F/ITS4), 18S-V4, 18S-V9, or custom primers.

Pipeline: cutadapt primer trimming → VSEARCH merge → maxEE 1.0 filter → dereplication → UNOISE3 → UCHIME3 → read mapping → SINTAX against UNITE v10.

Output: ASV table with taxonomy, per-sample read survival, Shannon diversity, composition charts, TSV/FASTA downloads.

DNA barcoding (Sanger ITS)

Input: FASTA pasted into the form (up to 50 sequences) or uploaded FASTA / .ab1 trace files.

Pipeline: quality trimming (Mott, Q20) → orientation → VSEARCH --usearch_global against UNITE species hypotheses (top 10 hits) → SINTAX confidence → uchime_ref chimera check.

Output: identity %, coverage %, SH, assignment tier (species ≥ 98.5 % identity & ≥ 90 % coverage; genus 97–98.5 %; family/order 90–97 %), printable report.

Before you submit

  • Everything is public. Uploaded reads, metadata (location, coordinates, submitter name) and all derived results are published openly (CC BY 4.0) in a public Drive folder and a public ledger. Do not upload data under embargo or containing personal information.
  • Accepted inputs: .fastq, .fq, .fastq.gz, .fq.gz (amplicon); .fasta, .fa, .fna, .fas, .txt, .ab1 (barcoding). Google Forms accepts up to 10 files per question and files up to 1 GB each; the free compute tier processes roughly up to {{ max_read_pairs_note }} comfortably.
  • Google sign-in. Google Forms requires you to be signed in to a Google account to upload files (a Google policy for file-upload questions). Your e-mail is recorded in the private form responses only and is not published.
  • Turnaround. Jobs run one at a time on a public GitHub Actions runner; barcoding takes 2–5 min, amplicon jobs 10–60 min depending on read counts. Keep the job id shown in the form confirmation, or find your job on the dashboard.
